HOW IT WORKS
-------------
The Header Menu in WP Admin > Menus is the single source of truth:

  Header Menu Structure:          Footer Sitemap Output:
  ─────────────────────           ──────────────────────
  About Us                   →    About Us (bold header)
    ├─ Board of Directors    →      Board of Directors (italic link)
    ├─ Finance & Investment  →      Finance & Investment (italic link)
    └─ Publications          →      Publications (italic link)
  Donors                     →    Donors (bold header)
    ├─ Giving Options        →      Giving Options (italic link)
    └─ Donor Stories         →      Donor Stories (italic link)
  ... etc.

Top-level items become column headers.
Child items become sub-links below each header.
Only 2 levels deep (parents + children).


REMOVING THE OLD FOOTER (AFTER APPROVAL)
------------------------------------------
Once the client approves the new dynamic footer, the old
widget-based footer can be removed:

1. In footer.php, delete the entire <div class="upper-footer">...</div> block
   (the section with all the dynamic_sidebar() calls)
2. Optionally remove the old sidebar registrations from functions.php:
   - footer-aboutus, footer-donors, footer-advisors,
     footer-nonprofits, footer-students, footer-impact
   (Keep footer-logo and footer-quicklinks — still in use)
3. Clear caches

Do NOT remove the old footer until the client has compared
both versions and confirmed they want the new one.


ROLLBACK PLAN
--------------
If something goes wrong:
1. Replace footer.php with the backup from Step 1
2. Replace functions.php with the backup from Step 1
3. Replace css/custom.css with the backup from Step 1
4. Delete template-parts/footer-sitemap.php (optional)
5. Clear all caches
6. The site will be restored to its previous state
