## Scope
Redesign and combine the Staff and Board pages into a single, polished page featuring a professional grid layout with hover or click-to-reveal bios for each team member. The layout will be flexible enough to accommodate changes in staff and board membership over time. Do this work on a draft page.

## Deliverables
- Design a unified Staff & Board page with a clean, professional grid layout
- Implement hover/click-to-reveal bio functionality for each person
- Build a flexible layout that accommodates fluctuating team sizes (up to 15 members per section)
- Display board member titles (Officer positions and Board Member designation) and locations
- Include placeholder image support for members without photos

## Staff Details
- Photos displayed in grid layout
- Hover-over or click reveals staff bio
- Must accommodate approximately 13-15 people
- Layout should not "break" when adding/removing staff members
- More polished, "adult" aesthetic requested
- Client will provide photos and bios

## Board Details
- Similar hover/click bio functionality as Staff section
- Display board member position (4 officer positions, rest listed as "Board Member")
- Include member location
- Must accommodate up to 15 members
- Some members may not have photos (use placeholder)

## Reference
- Design reference: [Housing Trust Silicon Valley staff/board page](https://housingtrustsv.org/about/staff-board/)
